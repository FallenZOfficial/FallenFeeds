## 🏕️ COOPERATIVE SURVIVOR CAMPS

> *Build together. Scavenge together. Survive together.*

### 🏕️ Establish Your Camp

* Create a persistent survivor camp.
* Invite teammates to join your camp.
* Manage your shared camp through the camp panel.

### 🧭 Expedition System

Send survivors on timed expeditions to gather resources:

* 🔎 Scavenging runs
* 🦌 Hunting trips
* 📦 Supply runs

**🎒 Claim Your Rewards**

* Transfer gathered materials to your personal stash.
* Claim expedition cash to your bank balance.

### 📦 Shared Camp Economy

* Donate materials to shared camp storage.
* Purchase shared rations.
* Recruit additional survivors.
* Invest resources into camp upgrades.

### 🛠️ Camp Upgrades

| Upgrade       | Benefit                          |
| ------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 🏠 Shelter    | Expands camp membership capacity |
| 🔧 Workshop   | Improves expedition yields       |
| 🗼 Watchtower | Reduces supply-run risk          |
| 🪖 Barracks   | Camp development upgrade         |
| 🌱 Garden     | Improves expedition yields       |

### 🤝 Build Your Legacy

Coordinate with your teammates, manage resources, upgrade your facilities, and develop a thriving survivor camp.

Every expedition is an opportunity. Every upgrade helps shape your survival.
